# 22. 90-Day Execution Plan (Phase 1 completion and market validation)

**Scope guard:**
- These 90 days complete **Phase 1 only**: validation, research, design, legal, commercial groundwork.
- **No Phase 2 work and no application code** (directive §14). Phase 2 starts only after founder approval of Phase 1 at the Day-90 gate, or earlier if the founder explicitly approves.
- This plan **supersedes** the engineering stream in Phase 0 §20, which assumed Phase 2 would run in parallel (conflict C2).

> **Founder review (18 Nov 2026):** the evidence items E-01–E-15 in [validation-evidence-register.md](validation-evidence-register.md) are the checklist for this plan. Phase 1 approval (P1-D12) remains **pending** until they are documented as met or explicitly waived.

## 22.1 Objectives by Day 90

1. **Validate or reject the first product** (Workspace RF) with evidence from at least 15 financial institutions.
2. **Sign 2–3 sandbox design-partner agreements**, paid or LOI.
3. **Close the regulatory unknowns** for the first product: the official BCT 2022-08 text and counsel answers Q13–Q18 (plus Phase 0 Q1–Q12).
4. **Complete the Government-track user research** from Phase 1 v1.0. Its kit is ready.
5. **Turn wireframes into a high-fidelity clickable prototype** for the first product and test it with design partners.
6. **Resolve D12 (brand)** and obtain native AR/FR review of key texts.
7. **Prepare the Phase 1 approval package**, including re-scored opportunities.

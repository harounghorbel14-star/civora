1. **The data-protection authority (INPDP) is reported as not operating** (Oct 2026), and Law 2004-63 may require **written consent and notice at least one month before** collecting interview data (Art. 27, 31). Ask counsel first (action 1) before the first interview.
2. **Workspace RF alone cannot fund the Moderate plan.** It would need about 80 Tunisian institutions to break even, against 32 relevant institutions ([20](20-updated-financial-assumptions.md) §20.7.7). It is the wedge, not the whole business case.
3. **STB is buying complaint-ticketing software now** (consultation CD 23/2026) ([competitor scan](validation/competitor-desk-scan.md)). Ask about it in the STB interview.

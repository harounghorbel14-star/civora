# 13. Partnership One-Pager (donors, prime contractors, integrators)

> For conversations with donor programme teams (e.g. GovTech, interoperability, social-protection or health digitisation programmes) and with prime contractors/integrators. Internal draft. Confirm every [P] fact before external use.

## CIVORA in one sentence
An independent Tunisian GovTech company that builds the **service layer** institutions need on top of national digital infrastructure: case management, citizen tracking, an AI guide on official content, and consent/access transparency. All of it is deployable on sovereign infrastructure in Arabic and French.

## What we bring to a programme or consortium

| Need in the programme | CIVORA contribution |
|---|---|
| Institutions connected to the interoperability platform still need usable back offices | **Workspace**: queues, checklists, SLAs, decisions, appointments, configured per service |
| Citizens need visible progress | Status tracking and notifications (white-label) |
| Procedure simplification needs citizen-facing explanation | **Guide**: multilingual AI answers grounded only in official content (Arabic, Derja, French, English) |
| Data-protection accountability | Consent receipts, citizen-visible access logs, tamper-evident audit |
| Local capacity and cost-effectiveness | Tunisian team; product-based delivery; knowledge transfer to institution and local integrators |

## How we work with partners
- **Subcontractor or consortium member** under the programme's procurement rules.
- **Open standards:** OpenID Connect, W3C Verifiable Credentials, FHIR for health, and X-Road/UXP-compatible integration through members' security servers.
- **No lock-in:**
  - open data formats
  - documented APIs
  - source-code escrow option
  - transferable operations
- **Open-core option** (decision D11 pending): adapters, catalogue schema and Derja evaluation datasets could be released as open source.

## Commitments
- Citizen Data Charter: no data sale, no advertising, data stays in-country, citizen-visible access logs, and AI that never decides eligibility.
- Anti-bribery and conflict-of-interest policies; compliance with donor integrity and sanctions rules.

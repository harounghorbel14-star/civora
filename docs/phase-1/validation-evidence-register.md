## Decision rules at the Day-90 gate

| Evidence outcome | Consequence |
|---|---|
| E-01, E-02, E-03, E-04 **Met** and no blocking finding in E-05/E-07 | P1-D3 confirmed. Funding target can be committed (P1-D7). Phase 1 eligible for approval (P1-D12) once E-09/E-10/E-11 are met or explicitly waived |
| E-01 Met but E-04 weak (no willingness to pay) | Re-score ([09](09-opportunity-scoring-matrix.md)); consider document processing (Phase 10 timing) or a Government-first donor route; **no funding commitment** |
| E-02 reveals blocking constraints (e.g. outsourcing approval that customers won't seek) | Shift to an on-premises licence model or revise the first product |
| E-11 confirms trademark conflict | Rename before any brand expenditure (P1-D13) |

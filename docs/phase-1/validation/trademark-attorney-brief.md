## 4. Deliverables to request

1. A clearance opinion per mark, with a risk grade (low / medium / high) per class and territory.
2. A filing strategy: national filing at INNORPI first, and an international extension route (the Madrid Protocol, if Tunisia's membership is confirmed [A]) for EU, Maghreb and GCC. Include timing and **cost estimates**.
3. An opinion on the **Arabic-script** mark and transliteration variants.
4. Advice on the CIVORA conflict (DKSR GmbH) and on stopping use of the working name.

## 5. Searches the founder can run directly (free, about 1 hour)

**INNORPI online database:**
1. Open www.innorpi.tn. The research environment could not open it (TLS error); a normal browser should work.
2. Go to the trademark search.
3. Search each variant in §3, all classes, then classes 9 / 35 / 38 / 42.
4. Screenshot the results page with the date.

**WIPO Global Brand Database:**
1. Open branddb.wipo.int and complete the human-verification check.
2. Search "KELORA" with fuzzy matching on.
3. Filter by designated office TN, then EM (EU), MA, SA, AE.
4. Repeat for "كيلورا" if Arabic search is available.
5. Export or screenshot the results.

**Domain:**
1. Request a price for **kelora.com** through the marketplace listing.
2. Check **kelora.tn** availability with an accredited .tn registrar.

**Record everything:**
- Add the results to the "trademark" section of the [quote comparison sheet](../data/quote-comparison.csv).
- Attach the screenshots to E-11. **Desk results do not replace the attorney's opinion.**

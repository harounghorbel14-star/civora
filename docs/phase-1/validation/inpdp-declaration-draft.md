# INPDP Declaration Preparation — CIVORA's Own Processing (draft)

|---|---|
| Purpose | Pre-filled content for the **prior declaration** of CIVORA's own personal-data processing (Law 2004-63 Art. 7), and a record of processing |

The official declaration form and procedure are set by decree (Art. 7, last paragraph) and were not obtained: the INPDP site was unreachable. The fields below follow the information list of **Art. 8** of the law, read in the official text [S168][S182]. A declaration under Art. 7 should need no more than this.

## Processing 3 — Staff and recruitment (once the company hires)

Employment processing that is necessary for work is exempt from Art. 7 and 8 by Art. 16 of the law. Counsel to confirm the scope. Record it in the internal register anyway.

## Filing checklist (founder)

1. Counsel answers **A1** (who files, before or after incorporation) and **A4** (INPDP status).
2. Complete the bracketed fields.
3. File by the means the law accepts:
   - deposit against receipt
   - registered letter with acknowledgement
   - any means leaving a written trace (Art. 7)
4. Keep proof of filing.
5. Per Art. 7, the absence of objection within **one month** counts as acceptance.
6. Start the research processing only when counsel confirms the Art. 7 / Art. 31 timing.
7. If the INPDP cannot receive the filing, keep evidence of every attempt and follow counsel's advice.

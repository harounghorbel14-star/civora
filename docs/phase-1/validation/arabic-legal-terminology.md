## 1. Why this matters

Customer-facing texts must use the **same words as the regulation**. This applies to the status page, acknowledgements, decision letters and mediator information. Earlier drafts used **شكاية** ("complaint") and **الموفّق المصرفي** ("banking mediator"). The official Arabic circular uses **شكوى / شكاوى** and **الموفّق البنكي**. These drafts are corrected in §4.

## 3. Discrepancies between the French and Arabic official texts (for counsel)

| ID | Article | French | Arabic | Effect | Question |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D1** | 2022-08 Art. 8 | "quinze (15) **jours ouvrables**" | "خمسة عشر (15) **يوم عمل مصرفي**" (*banking* working day) | Which calendar the deadline engine uses: bank holidays and banking closures, or general working days | **Q-12:** Which language version prevails? Is the 15-day count based on banking working days? |
| **D2** | 2022-08 Art. 10 | "L'**organe de direction** … soumet à l'**organe d'administration** un rapport annuel" (management → board) | "يحيل **هيكل الإدارة** للمؤسسة إلى **هيكل التسيير** تقريرا سنويا". On a literal reading, the administration body refers the report to the management body; the direction may be reversed | Who prepares and who receives the annual report | **Q-13:** Confirm the official Arabic equivalents of "organe d'administration" and "organe de direction" (defined in Circular 2021-05; its Arabic version is not on the BCT site) and the direction of the report |
| D3 | 2022-08 Art. 14 | "reporting comptable, prudentiel et **statistique**" | Art. 14: "التصريح المحاسبي والاحترازي **والمالي**"; the visa says "**والإحصائي**" | None (internal drafting variation) | — |

*Until counsel answers, the product follows the stricter reading:* banking working days, i.e. the earlier deadline when calendars differ. The board report is configurable in both directions.

## 5. Note on data-protection consent for research (Law 2004-63 Art. 27, 31)

Reading the Arabic and French texts side by side confirms two requirements that affect the founder's interviews and usability tests:

1. **Art. 27:** except where a law provides otherwise, processing requires the data subject's **express and written consent** (الموافقة الصريحة والكتابية). Consent can be withdrawn at any time. The consent forms in the [outreach kit](outreach-kit.md) and the [usability kit](usability-testing-kit.md) are therefore **written**, with a signature or written email confirmation.
2. **Art. 31:** after the INPDP declaration period (Art. 7, one month), the people concerned must be informed in writing of twelve listed points. That notice must be given **at least one month before** the date set for the processing. The participant information notices list all twelve points.

**The one-month notice in Art. 31 could delay the start of interviews and tests.** Counsel must say how it applies to short professional interviews before the first interview is held (question **Q-L1** in the [counsel brief](counsel-engagement-brief.md)).

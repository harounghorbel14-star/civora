# 16. Funding Strategy

> Fund names below are illustrative categories to research. **No fund, programme or investor has been contacted, and availability and terms must be verified** [U].

## 16.1 Funding philosophy

1. **Stage capital to evidence.** Each round funds the milestones that de-risk the next (Section 15.10).
2. **Prefer customer and donor money over investor money in Year 1.** Paid pilots and donor-funded subcontracts validate demand *and* fund development.
3. **Avoid funding sources that compromise neutrality or ethics.** Investors must accept the Citizen Data Charter (no data monetisation) as a binding constraint, ideally written into the shareholders' agreement.
4. **Keep government independence.** A government or state-owned investor may be acceptable later, but it creates procurement conflicts of interest and perceived capture. Evaluate case by case.

## 16.4 Investor narrative (draft, for Phase 1 refinement)

1. **Problem:** citizens face fragmented, slow procedures. Governments have front doors (apps, portals) but under-equipped back offices. Low-trust environments resist centralisation.
2. **Insight:** the winning model is sovereign, interoperable and modular. Value sits in service delivery on top of open digital public infrastructure, not in owning identity.
3. **Product:** Guide (AI on official sources) + Workspace (case management) + Trust (verification and e-signature) + Country Packs.
4. **Why now:**
   - mandatory interoperability in Tunisia [P][S26]
   - BCT e-KYC rules [P][S51]
   - health digitisation [P][S69]
   - EUDI wallets and open-DPI adoption globally [P][S102][S106]
5. **Why us:** Arabic/Derja/French native; sovereign-by-design; low cost base; security-first.
6. **Business model:** B2B recurring revenue funds a B2G pipeline; country cells scale internationally.
7. **Ask and milestones:** per stage (Section 15.10).
8. **Ethics as moat:** a Citizen Data Charter and transparency, which win trust from governments, civil society and donors.

## 16.5 Fundraising risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Investors want faster growth than GovTech allows | Show the L1 recurring line; stage expectations; target investors with GovTech experience |
| Investors push data monetisation | Charter in shareholders' agreement; select investors accordingly |
| FX/structure complexity deters foreign investors | Decide structure at seed with counsel (Section 13.9) |
| Grant dependency distracts from product | Cap grant-funded work at ~30% of engineering capacity [A] |

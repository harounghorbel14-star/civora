**Market reading:** the state is the dominant builder of citizen-facing digital government in Tunisia. The policy direction is clear and funded: interoperability, mobile identity, a unified app, a health record and procedure simplification. **The opportunity for a private company lies in delivery capacity, back-office modernisation, specialised components and regulated B2B services, not in a competing citizen front door.**

## 3.6 Market sizing (all [A] — to validate in the first 90 days)

**L1 — B2B trust services (Tunisia):**
- Volume: 250k–600k remote onboarding / re-KYC / e-signature events per year by 2028 across banks, payment institutions, telcos and insurers [A].
- Price: 2–4 TND per verification event, plus 2,000–8,000 TND per month platform fee per institution [A].
- **Serviceable market: ~1.5–4M TND per year.** This is modest. L1 is a cash-flow and credibility line, not the main prize.

**L2 — B2G platform & services (Tunisia):**
- Opportunity flow accessible to a young company: 3–8 opportunities per year at 0.3–3M TND each, so 2–15M TND per year in pipeline [A].
- At a 10–25% win rate: **0.3–3M TND per year** in contracts by Year 3 [A].

**L3 — International:** sized in Section 13.

**Conclusion:**
- Tunisia can sustain a profitable company of roughly 25–50 people once references exist.
- It cannot alone support venture-scale returns. International expansion (L3) is required for that, and Tunisian references are the prerequisite.

## 3.7 Market entry barriers

1. **References requirement in public tenders** and the risk-aversion of public buyers toward start-ups [A]. Mitigation: consortia with established integrators, donor projects, and B2B references first.
2. **Late payment on public contracts** [P][S115]. Mitigation: milestone billing, donor-funded contracts, and B2B revenue to carry working capital.
3. **Hosting constraints.** Public platforms must be hosted at CNI, sectoral centres or licensed operators [P][S46]; sensitive data in a private cloud or with G-Cloud-labelled providers [P][S42]. Mitigation: a deployable platform that is not SaaS-only, and partnerships with labelled hosts.
4. **Data access requires authorisation** (Section 11). Mitigation: a front-end/workflow value proposition independent of data access.
5. **Trust.** Mitigation: Citizen Data Charter, transparency, minimal data.

## 3.8 Demand drivers (tailwinds)

- Government's explicit 2026–2030 commitment to digital administration and mandatory interoperability [P][S26][S30].
- Regulatory pull in finance (BCT e-KYC rules) [P][S51][S53] and cybersecurity (mandatory audits, Decree-law 2023-17) [P][S39].
- Health digitisation programme [P][S69].
- Tunisia's national AI strategy 2026–2030 [P][S31] (commentators note a lack of operational detail [P][S32]).
- The expected new data-protection law (draft organic law 095/2025, pending) [P][S34]. It will raise compliance demand for privacy-engineering capabilities.
